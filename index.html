Our Premium Services

Every service uses professional-grade products and techniques — built for Canadian conditions.

Exterior Detailing

Complete exterior wash, clay bar treatment, hand polish, and protective wax application for a flawless Canadian-winter-safe finish.

From $149 CAD / session

Interior Deep Cleaning

Steam cleaning, fabric extraction, leather conditioning, dashboard treatment, and complete odour elimination — including salt residue removal.

From $179 CAD / session

Paint Correction

Multi-stage machine polishing to eliminate swirl marks, scratches, road debris marks, and winter oxidation from Canadian roads.

From $399 CAD / stage

Ceramic Coating

Professional 9H ceramic coating providing up to 5 years of hydrophobic protection — ideal against salt, ice, and UV damage in Canada.

From $799 CAD / coat

Headlight Restoration

Restore clarity to yellowed or hazy headlights with our professional wet-sand and UV-seal treatment.

From $89 CAD / pair

Full Detail Package

The complete experience — exterior, interior, paint correction, and coating in one comprehensive session. Our most popular package.
